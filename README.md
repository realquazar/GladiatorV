# :crossed_swords: Gladiator V

Want to work out or get stronger but don't know where to begin? Well we got good news for you! You can now get the best work out schedule in your very own server! Introducing Gladiator V, your personal discord bot work out assistant for gym and calisthenics.

**Features:**
- :scroll: Get an effective routine to workout
-  :comet:Work out everyday to get stronger using /startworkout
- :man_cartwheeling: Work out sessions get harder the more you progress
- 🧗: Climb all 10 levels of Gladiator V to unlock your dream physique and grow stronger
- :muscle: You can flex your skills to other members in your server using the /flex command
- :bangbang: You can add your very own custom workout routines!
- 🍽️: Get diet suggestions
- :notes: Get access to our very own youtube workout playlist!
- :computer: Access the commands in your very own server or use our website dashboard as well to add work out routines, flexes and more!

We wish you luck fellow discorder to your journey of getting stronger and better! Hope you enjoy and support us by voting for Gladiator V on top.gg!
